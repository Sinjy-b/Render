MODEL NOTES

A field explorer, not a survey instrument

The 640 m scene is georeferenced to Thomson’s Falls and rendered at one world unit per metre. Regional relief uses a 30 m-class SRTM-derived grid. The narrow waterfall face is below that grid’s effective resolving power, so the documented 74 m drop is imposed as a local geomorphic constraint.

ImageryEsri World Imagery, used as the surface texture and an RGB green-cover proxy for vegetation placement.
FeaturesRiver, falls, gorge, near-falls footpath and lookout are reconstructed from imagery; small objects are approximate.
WeatherSun, mist, fog and rain are interactive visual scenarios. They are not a live forecast.
Snow decisionSnow is intentionally omitted. The widely reported 2017 Nyahururu event was identified by Kenya’s meteorological authority as hail.
